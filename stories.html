CLAUDIA & GERSON

Gerson and Claudia’s wedding on August 7th, 2025, was the culmination of an eight-year relationship. Having known each other since 2017, the couple spent nearly a decade building a foundation of friendship and mutual support before deciding to marry. The ceremony took place on a warm summer day, surrounded by their close friends and family. For those in attendance, the event felt like a natural next step for a couple that had already navigated many of life’s milestones together. The transition from long-term partners to spouses was marked by a straightforward exchange of vows and rings, symbolizing a commitment they had essentially been living out for years. By marrying last August, Gerson and Claudia officially moved into a new chapter of their lives, turning an eight-year history into a permanent partnership. The day was less about a new beginning and more about formalizing the strong, steady bond they had developed over the previous eight years.

BRYAN & KEYLA

Keyla and Bryan’s wedding on July 24th, 2024, marked a significant milestone in a relationship that has spanned over two decades. Now in their 30s, the couple first met when they were just 12 years old. This long history means they have spent the majority of their lives together, evolving from childhood friends into life partners and navigating the complexities of adulthood as a team. A central part of their lives and their wedding celebration was their family. Keyla and Bryan are parents to twins—a boy and a girl. Their children were an integral part of the day, reflecting the couple's established life together. For Keyla and Bryan, the wedding was not the start of a household, but rather a formal celebration of the family unit they had already built over many years. The ceremony served as a straightforward transition from a long-term partnership to a legal marriage. Given that they have been together since adolescence, the event was a natural progression of their story. By formalizing their commitment last July, they solidified a bond that was already deeply rooted in decades of shared experiences, from their early school years to the responsibilities of raising twins in their 30s.
