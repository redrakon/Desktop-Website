More About Me

I’m a photographer driven by one thing above all else story. To me, photography isn’t just about capturing what something looks like, it’s about preserving what it felt like in that exact moment. The quiet anticipation before a proposal, the overwhelming joy of a wedding day, the laughter shared at a baby shower, or the energy of a live musical event—those are the moments that deserve to be remembered, not just seen. My work is centered around storytelling through images. I aim to document real emotions, genuine connections, and the little in-between moments that often go unnoticed but mean everything later. Anyone can take a posed photo, but I’m more interested in the natural, unfiltered moments—the ones that tell the full story without needing explanation. I’ve had the opportunity to photograph a wide range of events, including weddings, proposals, baby showers, and musical performances. Each of these experiences has shaped how I approach my work. Weddings have taught me to be attentive and present, ready to capture fleeting emotions that happen in seconds. Proposals have shown me the importance of timing and subtlety, preserving a once-in-a-lifetime moment without interrupting it. Baby showers remind me how meaningful it is to document new beginnings and the love surrounding them. And musical events have helped me capture movement, passion, and energy in a way that feels alive even in a still image. What connects all of these experiences is people. I believe every person has a story worth telling, and my goal is to reflect that in a way that feels honest and personal. I take time to understand the people I work with their personalities, their vision, and what matters most to them so that the final images feel authentic and meaningful. My style leans toward natural lighting, real emotion, and a slightly cinematic feel. I want my photos to look timeless, something you can come back to years later and still feel the same emotions all over again. I don’t aim for perfection I aim for truth. The imperfect, candid moments are often the ones that hold the most beauty. Photography, to me, is more than just a creative outlet it’s a way of preserving memories that might otherwise fade with time. It’s about giving people something they can hold onto, something that tells their story long after the moment has passed. At the end of the day, my goal is simple: to create images that don’t just show a moment, but let you relive it.
